Meet Andrew, the Man Behind Every Detail

Diamond Auto Spa is not a franchise. There is no rotating crew, no rush to squeeze in the next appointment, and no one cutting corners to hit a quota. It is Andrew, his family behind him, and a genuine belief that every car deserves to be treated with care.

Andrew has been passionate about cars for as long as he can remember. Over more than a decade of working on vehicles, he developed a standard that most detailers simply do not hold themselves to: the job is not finished until it is right. Not until it looks right. Not until it is something he would be proud to hand back.

"Most detailers rush through cars to maximize profits. But we see ourselves as artists, not just detailers. Here, your car will be treated like a mural: with precision, effort, and genuine care."

When he started Diamond Auto Spa, the goal was simple: build a detailing service that operates the way he always believed it should. No shortcuts. Honest pricing. Results that actually last. Seven days a week, he shows up to people's homes and driveways across Lake County fully equipped and ready to do the work properly, however long it takes.

How We Work

What Sets Diamond Auto Spa Apart

No Rushing, Ever

Andrew does not overbook to squeeze in extra appointments. Each vehicle gets the time it actually needs. Some jobs take four hours. Some take a full day. The job ends when it is done right, not when the clock runs out.

One Person, Full Accountability

Andrew handles your vehicle personally on every job. There is no rotating team, no employee whose name you will never learn. When you book with Diamond Auto Spa, you know exactly who is touching your car and what standard they hold themselves to.

Transparent Pricing

You get a clear price before any work begins. No surprise add-ons, no upselling, no bill that looks different than what was quoted. What Andrew quotes is what you pay.

Fully Mobile, Fully Equipped

No shop drop-off, no arranging a ride. Andrew arrives at your home, office, or wherever your vehicle is parked with professional polishers, steam cleaners, hot water extractors, and everything else needed. You provide nothing except the vehicle.

Built for Florida Conditions

Central Florida UV, humidity, hard water, pollen, and heat are not afterthoughts. Every service uses products and techniques designed specifically to hold up in conditions that destroy shortcuts. Work done here is meant to last.

A Real Community Business

Diamond Auto Spa is based in Eustis and serves the Lake County community it is part of. The customers here are neighbors, not transactions. That is a distinction that shows in how Andrew approaches every appointment.
