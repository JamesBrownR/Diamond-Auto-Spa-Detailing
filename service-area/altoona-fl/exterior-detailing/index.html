Vehicles parked along the SR-19 and CR-42 corridor in northeast Lake County deal with paint contamination that most detail shops rarely see. Pine resin from the Ocala National Forest canopy drops onto paint surfaces and bonds hard in Florida heat within hours. Tree sap from the same canopy layers on top and etches into clear coat if left between washes. Fine red clay dust from unpaved county roads settles across every panel and acts as a mild abrasive that builds up between washes, dulling the finish over time. Florida UV operates on all of it continuously on vehicles parked in open rural driveways with inconsistent shade coverage.

A full exterior detail addresses the complete combination. Decontamination removes the bonded organic and inorganic contamination that washing leaves behind. Machine polishing corrects the paint defects that have developed underneath it. A paint sealant or wax applied at the end provides a protective barrier against the ongoing forest corridor environment.

Our Process

1

Pre-Wash Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint and notes sap bonding, oxidation, scratch depth, and contamination level. Sets expectations for what the detail will achieve and identifies any areas that need extra attention before work begins.

2

Pre-Rinse and Wheel Work

Vehicle rinsed to remove loose debris. Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned first while iron remover dwells on painted surfaces. Prevents cross-contamination from brake dust onto clean paint.

3

Hand Wash

Full two-bucket hand wash using pH-neutral soap and microfiber wash media. Panels washed top to bottom. Dried with clean microfiber towels to avoid water spots from the mineral-heavy well water common on rural Altoona properties.

4

Decontamination

Iron remover applied to all painted panels. Clay bar worked across the entire paint surface to lift pine resin, tree sap, and road dust that washing leaves behind. Paint tested by touch before moving on.

5

Machine Polish

Dual-action polisher removes swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation panel by panel. Compound and pad selection matched to paint condition. Any areas needing additional passes addressed before moving forward.

6

Protection and Finish

Paint sealant or wax applied to all painted surfaces. Trim dressed. Glass cleaned. Final inspection under proper lighting to confirm results before the job is called complete.
