Diamond Auto Spa travels from Eustis to Apopka regularly with a full professional setup on every visit. Andrew arrives at your Apopka location and handles everything on-site. No drop-off, no shop visit. We can typically get you scheduled within a few days.

Apopka sits in northwest Orange County with large open subdivisions and very little canopy cover across most of the city. Vehicles parked along Rock Springs Road, Sheeler Road, and throughout the 32703 and 32712 zip codes absorb direct Florida UV all day without interruption. That sustained, unbroken UV exposure is the single biggest driver of paint oxidation and clear coat failure in the area. Unprotected clear coat oxidizes and fades measurably faster in Apopka\'s open parking conditions than the same vehicle would in a shaded environment or a cooler climate.

Ceramic coating bonds to the clear coat at a molecular level and creates a hard, UV-blocking surface layer that intercepts sunlight before it reaches the paint underneath. The coating also creates a chemically resistant surface that road tar, bird dropping acids, and brake dust contamination from US-441 and SR-429 commuter driving sit on top of rather than bonding into. The hydrophobic properties shed water and contaminants faster, making the vehicle easier to keep clean between washes and reducing the contact time that environmental threats get with the paint surface.

Every coating job includes full decontamination and paint correction before the product goes on. Ceramic coating locks in whatever the paint looks like at time of application. Getting the paint right first is non-negotiable.

Our Process

1

Paint Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint under proper lighting to identify UV oxidation level and the correction needed before coating. Sets the time expectation for the full job before any work begins.

2

Full Decontamination

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar on all painted surfaces. Road tar and iron deposits from commuter driving lifted completely. No coating can bond correctly to contaminated paint. This stage cannot be skipped.

3

Paint Correction

Machine polish removes swirl marks, UV oxidation, bird dropping etching, and light scratches. Level of correction matched to paint condition. Since the coating preserves whatever the paint looks like underneath, getting the paint right first is the most important part of the whole job.

4

IPA Wipe Down

All painted panels wiped with isopropyl alcohol to remove any remaining polish oils or residue. Paint inspected one final time under lighting before coating begins. If anything is not right, it gets addressed here.

5

Ceramic Coating Application

Coating applied panel by panel. Each section worked in small areas, allowed to flash, then leveled with a clean suede cloth. Work proceeds in a logical order across the entire vehicle.

6

Cure and Handoff

Andrew walks through cure requirements and maintenance instructions before leaving. Following the cure protocol correctly is what separates a coating that lasts 2 to 5 years from one that degrades in months.
