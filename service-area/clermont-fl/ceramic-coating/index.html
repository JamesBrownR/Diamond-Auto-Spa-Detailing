Ceramic Coating for Clermont Vehicles

Diamond Auto Spa travels from Eustis to Clermont regularly with a full professional setup on every visit. Andrew arrives at your Clermont location fully equipped. No drop-off, no shop visit, no coordinating a ride.

Clermont has a specific case for ceramic coating that is different from the lake cities to the north. The open suburban layout across the rolling hills of South Lake County means most vehicles park in driveways and lots with no canopy cover whatsoever. Full Central Florida UV, year-round, with nothing blocking it. Unprotected clear coat in that environment oxidizes and fades faster than most owners track because the change is gradual rather than sudden. Vehicles making daily commutes on US-27 and the Florida Turnpike add road tar and brake dust contamination that compounds the wear on unprotected paint.

A ceramic coating changes the equation for vehicles in that environment. The hardened SiO2 layer is the strongest UV barrier available short of paint protection film. It repels road contamination before it can bond, resists bird droppings and bug acids before they can etch, and makes every wash dramatically easier for years. For a Clermont vehicle that has nowhere shaded to park, the protection compounds over time in a way that wax and sealant simply cannot match.

What's Included

✓
Full Decontamination Wash

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar on all painted surfaces. All bonded surface contamination removed before any correction or coating work begins.

✓
Paint Correction

At minimum a single-stage machine polish to remove swirl marks and light defects. Ceramic coating locks in whatever the paint looks like at time of application. Correction is not optional.

✓
IPA Panel Wipe

All panels wiped with isopropyl alcohol after polishing to remove polish oils and residue. Coating will not bond correctly to paint that has not been fully stripped of surface oils.

✓
Ceramic Coating Application

Professional-grade SiO2 ceramic coating applied panel by panel using applicator blocks and suede cloth. Each panel leveled before moving to the next.

✓
Cure Period Guidance

Andrew walks you through the cure requirements before leaving. Typically 24 hours before moisture exposure and 7 to 14 days before a full wash. Following the cure protocol is what determines how long the coating holds.

✓
Maintenance Instructions

Guidance on how to wash a coated vehicle to maximize coating life. Simple adjustments to your wash routine make a significant difference in how long the coating performs.

Our Process

1

Paint Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint under proper lighting to identify the level of correction needed. Sets the time expectation for the job before any work begins.

2

Full Decontamination

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar on all painted surfaces. No coating can bond correctly to contaminated paint. This stage cannot be skipped.

3

Paint Correction

Machine polish to remove swirl marks, water etching, and oxidation. Level of correction matched to paint condition. The coating preserves whatever the paint looks like underneath it.

4

IPA Wipe Down

All painted panels wiped with isopropyl alcohol to remove any remaining polish oils. Paint inspected one final time under lighting before coating begins.

5

Ceramic Coating Application

Coating applied panel by panel. Each section worked in small areas, allowed to flash, then leveled with a clean suede cloth. No panel is rushed.

6

Final Inspection and Cure Briefing

Vehicle inspected under lighting for any high spots or missed areas. Cure instructions reviewed with you before Andrew leaves.
