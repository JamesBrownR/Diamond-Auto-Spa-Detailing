What's Included

Two-Bucket Hand Wash

Wheel and Tire Cleaning

Iron Decontamination

Chemical iron remover applied to all painted surfaces. Dissolves embedded metallic brake dust and road fallout from daily commuting on US-27 and the Turnpike that washing alone cannot remove.

Clay Bar Treatment

Paint clayed to remove bonded surface contamination including road tar, bug residue, and industrial fallout. After claying, paint feels glass-smooth to the touch.

Machine Polish

Dual-action polisher removes light swirl marks, water etching, and UV oxidation. Restores clarity and depth to the paint. Actual correction work, not just a hand wax.

Our Process

1

Pre-Wash Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint before starting. Notes existing scratches, heavy contamination, water etching, and oxidation. Sets expectations for what the detail will achieve based on actual paint condition.

2

Pre-Rinse and Wheel Work

Vehicle rinsed to remove loose debris. Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned first while iron remover dwells on painted surfaces. Prevents cross-contamination from brake dust onto clean paint.

3

Hand Wash

Full two-bucket hand wash using pH-neutral soap and microfiber wash media. Panels washed top to bottom. Final rinse and dry with clean microfiber towels to avoid water spots.

4

Decontamination

Iron remover applied to all painted panels. Clay bar worked across the entire paint surface. Paint tested by touch before moving on. Glass-smooth is the target before any polishing begins.

5

Machine Polish

Dual-action polisher with appropriate pad and compound removes swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation. Work done panel by panel. Level of correction matched to paint condition and customer goals.

6

Protection and Finishing

Paint sealant or carnauba wax applied. Trim dressed. Glass cleaned. Final walk-around inspection. Vehicle handed back in better condition than it has been in years.
