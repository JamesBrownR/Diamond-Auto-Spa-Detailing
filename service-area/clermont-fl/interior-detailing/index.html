Our Process

1

Pre-Clean Assessment

Andrew walks through the interior before starting, notes problem areas, and confirms the scope of work so there are no surprises.

2

Dry Extraction

Full vacuum of all surfaces, crevices, under seats, and trunk. Pet hair removed with specialized tools before any wet work begins.

3

Steam Treatment

High-temperature steam applied to all hard surfaces. Kills bacteria, loosens grime, and sanitizes without chemicals.

4

Stain Pre-Treatment

Individual stains treated with targeted products before the main extraction pass. Giving treatments time to work produces dramatically better results.

5

Hot Water Extraction

Professional extractor used on all carpets, floor mats, and fabric seats. Hot water and cleaning solution injected into fibers, immediately extracted along with dissolved dirt and stains.

6

Surface Detail and Dress

All hard surfaces wiped down and dressed. Leather cleaned and conditioned. Glass cleaned streak-free. Final inspection before calling the job done.
