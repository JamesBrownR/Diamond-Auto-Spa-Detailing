Detailing Services Available in Eustis

Full Exterior Detail

Hand wash, iron decontamination, clay bar treatment, machine polish, and paint sealant or carnauba wax. Removes embedded contaminants that regular washing misses entirely. Your paint will have mirror-like clarity when we are done.

Interior Deep Clean

Steam cleaning, hot water extraction, carpet and upholstery shampooing, leather conditioning, dashboard and console wipe-down, and full door jamb cleaning. Stains, pet hair, odors, and years of grime handled in a single appointment.

Ceramic Coating

The highest level of paint protection available. A professionally applied ceramic coating bonds to your clear coat and delivers years of defense against UV rays, oxidation, bird droppings, tree sap, and water spots. A true game-changer for Florida vehicle owners.

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirl marks, light scratches, water etching, and oxidation. Andrew has been known to spend 8 or more hours on a single correction job, because that is what it sometimes takes to get the paint truly right.

Headlight Restoration

Florida's UV destroys plastic headlight lenses faster than almost anywhere in the country. We wet-sand, compound, polish, and seal headlights back to crystal clarity at a fraction of the cost of replacement lenses.

Engine Bay Detail

A clean engine bay makes spotting leaks easier, helps components run cooler, and looks sharp under the hood. We safely degrease, rinse, and dress all engine bay surfaces without damaging electronics or seals.
