Detailing Services Available in Eustis

Full Exterior Detail

Hand wash, iron decontamination, clay bar treatment, machine polish, and paint sealant or carnauba wax. Removes embedded contaminants regular washing misses. Mirror-like clarity when done.

Interior Deep Clean

Steam cleaning, hot water extraction, carpet and upholstery shampooing, leather conditioning, dashboard wipe-down, and door jamb cleaning. Stains, pet hair, and odors handled in one appointment.

Ceramic Coating

Bonds to your clear coat and delivers years of defense against UV rays, oxidation, bird droppings, tree sap, and water spots. The highest level of paint protection available for Florida vehicles.

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirl marks, light scratches, water etching, and oxidation. Andrew has been known to spend 8 or more hours on a single correction job because that is what it takes to do it right.

Headlight Restoration

Florida's UV destroys plastic headlight lenses faster than almost anywhere in the country. We wet-sand, compound, polish, and seal them back to crystal clarity at a fraction of the replacement cost.

Engine Bay Detail

A clean engine bay makes spotting leaks easier, helps components run cooler, and looks sharp under the hood. We safely degrease, rinse, and dress all surfaces without damaging electronics or seals.
