What's Included

✓
Full Decontamination Wash

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar before any polishing begins. Contaminated paint causes new scratches when polished. This step cannot be skipped.

✓
Paint Thickness Measurement

Clear coat thickness measured across all panels before starting. Identifies thin areas from previous polishing or body repair. Guides how aggressively each area can be worked safely.

✓
Paint Defect Assessment

Paint inspected under proper lighting to map swirl marks, scratches, water etching, and oxidation. Determines the number of correction stages required and sets accurate expectations before work begins.

✓
Multi-Stage Machine Polishing

Dual-action or rotary polisher with cutting compound followed by progressively finer polish stages. Each stage removes the marks left by the previous one. Number of stages matched to paint condition.

✓
Panel-by-Panel Work

Repair performed one panel at a time with the machine set to the correct speed and pressure for that panel. Andrew adjusts throughout rather than running a single pass across the whole car.

✓
Final Inspection Under Lighting

Completed paint inspected under dedicated lighting at multiple angles. Any remaining defects addressed before the job is called done.

✓
Paint Protection Application

Repaired paint finished with a paint sealant or wax. If ceramic coating is being applied as a follow-on service, IPA wipe-down is performed instead and coating follows.

Repair Stages Explained

1

Single-Stage Repair

One pass with a cutting compound removes light swirl marks, minor water spots, and light oxidation. Suitable for vehicles in reasonably good condition. Typically used as prep for ceramic coating on well-maintained vehicles or as a standalone service on paint that just needs a refresh.

2

Two-Stage Repair

Heavier cutting compound addresses deeper swirl marks, water etching, and moderate oxidation. Finishing polish then refines the surface. Vehicles that have been using automatic car washes and parking under the Eustis tree canopy for several seasons typically need two-stage repair to fully restore clarity.

3

Multi-Stage Repair

Three or more stages for heavily defected paint, severe oxidation, deep water etching, or neglected vehicles. Andrew has spent a full day on a single repair job because the paint required it. This produces show-quality results on vehicles that looked beyond saving.
