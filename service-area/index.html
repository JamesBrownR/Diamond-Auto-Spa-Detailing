Service Area FAQs

How far do you travel for appointments?

We're based in Eustis and typically serve within a 30–40 minute radius. This covers most of Lake County, parts of Orange County (Apopka), and select Pinellas County locations like Madeira Beach. Call us if you're unsure — we'll tell you straight.

Is there a travel fee for areas farther from Eustis?

For locations significantly outside our primary service area, a small travel fee may apply. We'll be upfront about this when you call for a quote — no surprises.

What do you need at the location?

Ideally a flat surface with enough room to walk around the vehicle, and access to a standard outdoor water spigot if available. If water access is limited, let us know — we can work around most situations.

How do I book?

Call or text 352-931-4860 or use the quote form on our homepage. We respond quickly and can usually get you on the schedule within a few days.
