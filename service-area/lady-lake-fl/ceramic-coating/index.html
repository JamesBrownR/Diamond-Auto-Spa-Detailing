Lady Lake vehicles deal with two specific paint threats that ceramic coating addresses directly. US-27 and US-441 through the area are among the busiest roads in Central Florida and generate heavy bug accumulation on front panels, hoods, and windshields. Bug residue is acidic and bonds to paint quickly in Florida heat. On unprotected clear coat, it begins to etch within days of exposure during the warm months. Ceramic coating creates a hard, chemically resistant surface layer that bug acids sit on top of rather than penetrating to the clear coat underneath, making them significantly easier to remove at the next wash and preventing the etching from occurring.

Open driveway parking along Rolling Acres Road and throughout the 32159 zip code means full Florida UV exposure all day with no canopy cover to slow it down. Florida UV is among the most intense in the country and oxidizes unprotected clear coat faster than almost any other climate. Ceramic coating blocks UV before it reaches the paint, intercepting the primary driver of long-term clear coat failure in the Lady Lake environment.

Every coating job includes full decontamination and paint correction before the product goes on. Ceramic coating locks in whatever the paint looks like at time of application. Getting the paint right first is non-negotiable.

Our Process

1

Paint Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint under proper lighting to identify bug etching on front panels, UV oxidation, and the level of correction needed before coating. Sets the time expectation for the full job before any work begins.

2

Full Decontamination

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar on all painted surfaces. Bonded bug residue and road contamination from US-27 and US-441 lifted completely. No coating can bond correctly to contaminated paint. This stage cannot be skipped.

3

Paint Correction

Machine polish removes swirl marks, bug etching on front panels, UV oxidation, and light scratches. Level of correction matched to paint condition. Since the coating preserves whatever the paint looks like underneath, getting the paint right first is the most important part of the whole job.

4

IPA Wipe Down

All painted panels wiped with isopropyl alcohol to remove any remaining polish oils or residue. Paint inspected one final time under lighting before coating begins. If anything is not right, it gets addressed here.

5

Ceramic Coating Application

Coating applied panel by panel. Each section worked in small areas, allowed to flash, then leveled with a clean suede cloth. Work proceeds in a logical order across the entire vehicle.

6

Cure and Handoff

Andrew walks through cure requirements and maintenance instructions before leaving. Following the cure protocol correctly is what separates a coating that lasts 2 to 5 years from one that degrades in months.
