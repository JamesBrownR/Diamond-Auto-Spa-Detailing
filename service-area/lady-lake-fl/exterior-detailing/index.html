US-27 and US-441 through the Lady Lake area are among the busiest stretches of road in Central Florida. Vehicles that travel these corridors regularly accumulate heavy bug accumulation on front panels, hoods, and windshields. Bug residue is acidic and bonds to paint quickly in Florida heat. Left in place for even a few days in Central Florida sun, it begins to etch into clear coat. Road tar and iron contamination from high-traffic driving also embed into paint surfaces over time and require iron decontamination and clay bar treatment to remove properly.

Lady Lake properties along Rolling Acres Road and throughout the 32159 zip code generally sit in open driveways without canopy cover. The combination of corridor bug accumulation, embedded road contamination, and open-sky UV is what a full exterior detail addresses from start to finish.

Our Process

1

Pre-Wash Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint and notes bug etching on front panels, road tar bonding, UV oxidation, and swirl marks. Sets expectations for what the detail will achieve and identifies areas needing extra attention before work begins.

2

Pre-Rinse and Wheel Work

Vehicle rinsed to remove loose debris. Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned first while iron remover dwells on painted surfaces. Prevents cross-contamination from brake dust and road grime onto clean paint.

3

Hand Wash

Full two-bucket hand wash using pH-neutral soap and microfiber wash media. Panels washed top to bottom and dried with clean microfiber towels to avoid water spots.

4

Decontamination

Iron remover applied to all painted panels. Clay bar worked across the entire paint surface to lift bonded bug residue, road tar, and embedded contamination that washing leaves behind. Paint tested by touch before moving on. Front panels on US-27 corridor vehicles often require extra passes.

5

Machine Polish

Dual-action polisher removes swirl marks, bug etching, UV oxidation, and light scratches panel by panel. Compound and pad selection matched to paint condition. Any areas needing additional passes addressed before moving forward.

6

Protection and Finish

Paint sealant or wax applied to all painted surfaces. Trim dressed. Glass cleaned streak-free including windshield bug residue. Final inspection under proper lighting before the job is called complete.
