Our Process

1

Pre-Clean Assessment

Andrew walks through the interior before starting, notes problem areas including heat-baked staining, leather condition, pet hair, and odor sources, and confirms the scope of work so there are no surprises.

2

Dry Extraction

Full vacuum of all surfaces, crevices, under seats, and trunk. Specialized tools used to lift pet hair before any wet work begins. Starting dry prevents wet contamination from spreading during the cleaning stages.

3

Steam Treatment

High-temperature steam applied to all hard surfaces. Kills bacteria and mold, loosens grime, and sanitizes without chemicals. Most impactful on dashboard and console surfaces that have absorbed months of intense heat in open-sun parking.

4

Stain Pre-Treatment

Individual stains treated with targeted products and allowed to dwell. Heat-baked contamination needs proper contact time before extraction can pull it fully from carpet and fabric fibers.

5

Hot Water Extraction

Professional extractor worked through all carpets, seats, and mats. Multiple passes on heavily soiled areas. The extractor injects and immediately recovers, lifting dissolved contamination out of the fibers rather than spreading it around.

6

Surface Wipe-Down and Dress

All hard surfaces wiped clean and dressed. Leather cleaned and conditioned. Glass cleaned streak-free. Door jambs and pockets finished. Final inspection before calling the job complete.
