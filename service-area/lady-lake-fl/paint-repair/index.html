What's Included

✓
Full Decontamination Wash

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar before any polishing begins. Contaminated paint causes new scratches when polished. This step cannot be skipped.

✓
Paint Thickness Measurement

Clear coat thickness measured across all panels before starting. Identifies thin areas from previous polishing or body repair. Guides how aggressively each area can be worked safely.

✓
Paint Defect Assessment

Paint inspected under proper lighting to map swirl marks, scratches, water etching, and oxidation. Determines the number of repair stages required and sets accurate expectations before work begins.

✓
Multi-Stage Machine Polishing

Dual-action or rotary polisher with cutting compound followed by progressively finer polish stages. Each stage removes the marks left by the previous one. Number of stages matched to paint condition.

✓
Panel-by-Panel Work

Repair performed one panel at a time with machine set to the correct speed and pressure for each area. Adjusted throughout rather than running a single pass across the whole car.

✓
Final Inspection Under Lighting

Completed paint inspected under dedicated lighting at multiple angles. Any remaining defects addressed before the job is called done.

✓
Paint Protection Application

Repaired paint finished with a paint sealant or wax. If ceramic coating is being applied as a follow-on service, IPA wipe-down is performed instead and coating follows.

Repair Stages Explained

1

Single-Stage Repair

One pass with a cutting compound removes light swirl marks, minor water spots, and light oxidation. Suitable for well-maintained vehicles or as prep for ceramic coating on paint in reasonable condition.

2

Two-Stage Repair

Heavier cutting compound addresses deeper swirl marks from US-27 and US-441 car washes, moderate UV oxidation from the open residential layout, and water etching from Florida storm mineral deposits. Finishing polish refines the surface. The most common approach for Lady Lake vehicles that have been here a few seasons.

3

Multi-Stage Repair

Three or more stages for heavily defected paint, severe oxidation, or older vehicles that have accumulated years of Florida exposure without professional paint care. Well-maintained older vehicles common in The Villages area are strong candidates when owners want to restore the paint to its best possible condition.
