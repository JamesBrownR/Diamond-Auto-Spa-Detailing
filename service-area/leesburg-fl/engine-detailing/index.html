Leesburg sits in the middle of a chain-of-lakes environment with Lake Harris and Lake Griffin flanking the city, and the ambient humidity is consistently elevated year-round. Engine bays here retain moisture between drives longer than inland areas, which accelerates corrosion on metal brackets, fittings, and fasteners and encourages mold growth on rubber hoses and plastic covers. Florida heat compounds the problem by baking grease and oil residue onto every surface faster than in cooler climates. Organic debris from the heavy oak canopy along Silver Lake and Dixie Avenue finds its way under hoods through air intake gaps and adds to the buildup over time.

Engine detailing removes all of it safely. Electrical components are protected before any water is introduced. Degreaser breaks down the buildup with proper dwell time. Dedicated brushes work through every accessible area. A controlled low-pressure rinse removes the dissolved contamination. All plastic and rubber surfaces are dressed when dry to slow further deterioration from lake humidity and heat.

What's Included

✓
Electrical Protection

Sensitive electrical components, fuse boxes, air intakes, and exposed connectors covered and protected before any water or degreaser is introduced.

✓
Degreaser Application and Dwell

Professional-grade degreaser applied across all surfaces and allowed to dwell. Heat-baked grease and humidity-set deposits both need proper time to break down before agitation can remove them effectively.

✓
Brush Agitation

Multiple dedicated brushes worked through the entire bay. Firewall, strut towers, battery tray, around the engine block, along hose and wiring runs, and into every accessible recess.

✓
Low-Pressure Rinse

Controlled low-pressure rinse removes dissolved grease and degreaser residue without forcing water into sensitive areas. Not a pressure washer blast. Controlled and methodical throughout.

✓
Dry Down

Compressed air removes standing water from tight areas, wiring channels, and component gaps. Bay allowed to fully dry before any dressing is applied.

✓
Dressing and Protection

All plastic covers, rubber hoses, and trim pieces dressed with a non-greasy protectant. Particularly important in Leesburg's chain-of-lakes environment where persistent humidity accelerates rubber and plastic degradation between services.

Our Process

1

Pre-Clean Assessment

Andrew opens the hood and evaluates the level of buildup, identifies any visible leaks or concerns, and covers all sensitive electrical components and intakes before starting. Any concerns flagged to the customer before work begins.

2

Degreaser Application and Dwell

Degreaser applied across all surfaces. Heat-baked deposits and humidity-set contamination from the Leesburg lake environment both get appropriate dwell time to break down before agitation begins.

3

Agitation

Dedicated brushes worked through the entire bay. Firewall, strut towers, battery tray area, around the engine block, along hose and wiring runs, and into every accessible recess. The goal is to lift every accessible deposit before rinsing.

4

Rinse

Controlled low-pressure rinse removes dissolved contamination and degreaser. Water directed away from sensitive components. A second degreaser pass applied to any areas that did not fully clean on the first round.

5

Dry and Dress

Compressed air removes standing water from tight areas. Bay dried thoroughly before dressing begins. Protectant applied to all plastic and rubber surfaces. Final inspection to confirm everything is clean and all protective coverings removed.
