Leesburg vehicles deal with a three-part paint threat that compounds quickly without regular professional attention. The heavy oak canopy along Silver Lake and the residential streets off Dixie Avenue deposits pollen onto paint surfaces constantly. Oak pollen contains mild acids that etch into clear coat when baked in by Florida heat and left between washes, even for a few days. The elevated ambient humidity from Lake Harris and Lake Griffin traps contaminants against the paint surface and keeps it wet longer after rain, accelerating oxidation faster than it would in a drier environment. Florida UV then operates on all of it continuously, breaking down the clear coat from above while the contaminants work from the surface inward.

A full exterior detail addresses each layer of the problem. Clay bar decontamination lifts the bonded organic material that washing cannot reach. Machine polishing corrects the pollen etching, water spots from lake humidity mineral content, and swirl marks from US-27 automatic car washes that have built up over time. A paint sealant applied at the end creates a protective barrier against all three ongoing threats between details.

Our Process

1

Pre-Wash Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint and notes pollen etching, oxidation, swirl depth, and water spotting. Sets expectations for what the detail will achieve and identifies areas that need extra attention before work begins.

2

Pre-Rinse and Wheel Work

Vehicle rinsed to remove loose debris including pollen and road dust. Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned first while iron remover dwells on painted surfaces. Prevents cross-contamination from brake dust onto clean paint.

3

Hand Wash

Full two-bucket hand wash using pH-neutral soap and microfiber wash media. Panels washed top to bottom and dried with clean microfiber towels to avoid water spots from the mineral content in Leesburg area water.

4

Decontamination

Iron remover applied to all painted panels. Clay bar worked across the entire paint surface to lift bonded oak pollen, organic debris, and road contamination that washing leaves behind. Paint tested by touch before moving on.

5

Machine Polish

Dual-action polisher removes swirl marks, pollen etching, water spots, and oxidation panel by panel. Compound and pad selection matched to paint condition. Any areas needing additional passes addressed before moving forward.

6

Protection and Finish

Paint sealant or wax applied to all painted surfaces. Trim dressed. Glass cleaned streak-free. Final inspection under proper lighting before the job is called complete.
