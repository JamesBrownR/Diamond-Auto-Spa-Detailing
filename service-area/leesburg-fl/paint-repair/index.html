Leesburg paint develops a specific combination of defects over time. The heavy oak canopy along Silver Lake and residential streets off Dixie Avenue deposits pollen that bonds to clear coat quickly in Florida heat and lake-area humidity. The organic acids that pollen carries etch into the clear coat surface gradually — a process that accelerates in the elevated humidity from Lake Harris and Lake Griffin that keeps paint surfaces damp between rain events. Afternoon storms drop mineral-laden water that bakes into paint within the hour. The US-27 corridor adds road tar and brake dust to front panels with every drive. Most Leesburg vehicles that have been here a few seasons carry all of these in combination.

Paint repair removes all of it. Machine polishing physically abrades away the damaged outer layer of clear coat and levels the surface back to a flat, reflective finish. Under direct sunlight the difference is stark. Andrew finishes every job with a full inspection under proper lighting before calling it done.

What's Included

✓
Full Decontamination Wash

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar before any polishing begins. Contaminated paint causes new scratches when polished. This step cannot be skipped.

✓
Paint Thickness Measurement

Clear coat thickness measured across all panels before starting. Identifies thin areas from previous polishing or body repair. Guides how aggressively each area can be worked safely.

✓
Paint Defect Assessment

Paint inspected under proper lighting to map swirl marks, scratches, water etching, and oxidation. Determines the number of repair stages required and sets accurate expectations before work begins.

✓
Multi-Stage Machine Polishing

Dual-action or rotary polisher with cutting compound followed by progressively finer polish stages. Each stage removes the marks left by the previous one. Number of stages matched to paint condition.

✓
Panel-by-Panel Work

Repair performed one panel at a time with machine set to the correct speed and pressure for each area. Adjusted throughout rather than running a single pass across the whole car.

✓
Final Inspection Under Lighting

Completed paint inspected under dedicated lighting at multiple angles. Any remaining defects addressed before the job is called done.

✓
Paint Protection Application

Repaired paint finished with a paint sealant or wax. If ceramic coating is being applied as a follow-on service, IPA wipe-down is performed instead and coating follows.

Repair Stages Explained

1

Single-Stage Repair

One pass with a cutting compound removes light swirl marks, minor water spots, and light oxidation. Suitable for well-maintained vehicles or as prep for ceramic coating on paint that is in reasonable condition.

2

Two-Stage Repair

Heavier cutting compound addresses deeper swirl marks, oak pollen etching from the Silver Lake and Dixie Avenue canopy, and moderate oxidation. Finishing polish then refines the surface. The most common approach for Leesburg vehicles that have been parked under the canopy for several seasons without professional paint care.

3

Multi-Stage Repair

Three or more stages for heavily defected paint, severe oxidation, or deeply neglected vehicles. Andrew has spent a full day on a single repair job because the paint required it. This produces show-quality results on vehicles that looked beyond saving.
