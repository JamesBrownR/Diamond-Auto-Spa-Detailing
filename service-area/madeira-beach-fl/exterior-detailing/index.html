Coastal vehicles kept near the Gulf of Mexico deal with a combination of paint threats that inland vehicles rarely face together. Salt air from the Gulf carries chloride particles that embed into unprotected clear coat over time and accelerate oxidation from within. Gulf Coast UV is among the most intense in the country and breaks down the clear coat surface faster than inland sun. Constant coastal humidity keeps paint surfaces wet longer after rain and marine mist, giving embedded chlorides more contact time with the paint. The combined effect produces flat, hazy, oxidized paint on vehicles kept near the water for more than a season or two without protection.

A full exterior detail addresses the complete picture. Iron decontamination and clay bar treatment remove embedded salt and surface contamination that washing alone cannot reach. Machine polishing corrects the oxidation and defects that have developed. A paint sealant applied at the end creates a protective barrier that slows the return of coastal paint damage between details.

Our Process

1

Pre-Wash Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint and notes salt-accelerated oxidation, contamination level, scratch depth, and water etching. Sets expectations for what the detail will achieve and identifies areas needing extra attention before work begins.

2

Pre-Rinse and Wheel Work

Vehicle rinsed to remove loose salt residue and debris. Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned first while iron remover dwells on painted surfaces. Prevents cross-contamination from brake dust and salt residue onto clean paint.

3

Hand Wash

Full two-bucket hand wash using pH-neutral soap and microfiber wash media. Panels washed top to bottom and dried with clean microfiber towels to avoid water spots from coastal mineral content.

4

Decontamination

Iron remover applied to all painted panels. Clay bar worked across the entire paint surface to lift embedded salt contamination and surface deposits that washing leaves behind. Paint tested by touch before moving on. Coastal vehicles require more thorough clay work than inland vehicles at the same mileage.

5

Machine Polish

Dual-action polisher removes swirl marks, water etching, and salt-accelerated oxidation panel by panel. Compound and pad selection matched to paint condition. Any areas needing additional passes addressed before moving forward.

6

Protection and Finish

Paint sealant applied to all painted surfaces. Trim dressed. Glass cleaned including salt film from exterior surfaces. Final inspection under proper lighting before the job is called complete.
