Vehicle interiors near the Gulf of Mexico develop problems specific to the coastal environment. Salt air and constant humidity work into the interior through doors, vents, and seams over time. The combination of moisture and salt content accelerates mold and mildew growth in carpet and seat fabric faster than it would anywhere inland. Metal trim pieces, hardware, and exposed fasteners corrode. The musty odors that develop in vehicles kept near the water become persistent if not addressed at the source rather than masked. Sand tracked in from the beach works into carpet fibers and seat fabric and acts as an abrasive over time.

Steam cleaning kills mold and mildew at the source and sanitizes hard surfaces without chemicals. Hot water extraction removes the contamination from carpet and fabric fibers and the salt residue that has settled into upholstery over time. Leather conditioning restores and protects surfaces that the combination of salt air and Florida heat deteriorates faster near the coast than anywhere inland.

Our Process

1

Pre-Clean Assessment

Andrew walks through the interior before starting, notes problem areas including mildew growth, salt staining, sand accumulation, and odor sources, and confirms the scope of work so there are no surprises.

2

Dry Extraction

Full vacuum of all surfaces, crevices, under seats, and trunk. Sand removed before any wet work begins. Starting dry prevents wet contamination from spreading during the cleaning stages.

3

Steam Treatment

High-temperature steam applied to all hard surfaces. Kills mold and mildew bacteria, loosens grime, and sanitizes without chemicals. The most important step for coastal vehicles where salt humidity promotes bacterial growth between washes.

4

Stain Pre-Treatment

Individual stains treated with targeted products and allowed to dwell. Salt-accelerated staining and moisture damage in coastal upholstery needs proper contact time before extraction can pull it fully from the fibers.

5

Hot Water Extraction

Professional extractor worked through all carpets, seats, and mats. Multiple passes on areas with salt residue or moisture damage. The extractor injects and immediately recovers, lifting dissolved contamination out of the fibers rather than spreading it around.

6

Surface Wipe-Down and Dress

All hard surfaces wiped clean and dressed. Salt film removed from all trim and hardware. Leather conditioned. Glass cleaned streak-free including interior windshield haze from coastal air. Door jambs and pockets finished. Final inspection before calling the job complete.
