Detailing Services Available in Mount Dora

Full Exterior Detail

Hand wash, iron decontamination, clay bar treatment, polish, and paint sealant or wax. Your paint will reflect like glass when we're done. Perfect for vehicles that spend time in Florida's harsh sun.

Interior Deep Clean

Steam cleaning, hot water extraction, carpet and upholstery shampooing, door jamb cleaning, and full wipe-down of every surface. Odors, stains, and years of buildup gone in a single appointment.

Ceramic Coating

The highest level of paint protection available. A professionally applied ceramic coating repels water, UV rays, bird droppings, and road grime for years — not weeks. Ideal for new or freshly corrected paint.

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, and oxidation. Restores depth and clarity to paint that looks faded or dull. Andrew has been known to spend 8+ hours on a single correction job.

Headlight Restoration

Cloudy, yellowed headlights reduce visibility and drag down your vehicle's appearance. We wet-sand, polish, and seal headlights back to crystal clarity — at a fraction of replacement cost.

Engine Bay Detail

A clean engine bay makes spotting leaks easier, keeps components cooler, and looks impressive under the hood. We safely degrease, rinse, and dress all engine bay surfaces.

All services are performed mobile — we bring every tool, product, and supply needed. You don't need to do anything except let us know where to park.

Do you serve all of Mount Dora?

How long does a full detail take?

It depends on the service and the condition of your vehicle. A basic exterior detail typically takes 2–3 hours. A full interior and exterior with paint correction can take a full day. We'd rather take the time to do it right than rush and cut corners.

Do I need to be home during the appointment?

Not necessarily. Many customers leave the keys and go about their day. As long as we have access to the vehicle and know where to park, we're good. We'll send photos and updates when we're done.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Florida's climate?

Absolutely — arguably more so in Florida than anywhere else. The UV index in Central Florida is brutal, and ceramic coating provides the strongest barrier available against UV damage, oxidation, and paint fade. It also makes washing dramatically easier by preventing dirt and grime from bonding to the paint.
