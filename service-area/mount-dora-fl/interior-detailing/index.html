Mount Dora interiors face a specific set of conditions. The humidity that rolls in off Lake Dora and Lake Gertrude is particularly hard on fabric seats and carpets, creating ideal conditions for mold and mildew growth in vehicles that get damp and stay that way. The tree canopy over downtown and the historic neighborhoods near Donnelly Street is beautiful but deposits pollen and organic debris into open windows and ventilation systems constantly. Florida heat bakes odors into upholstery in a way that regular cleaning never fully addresses.

A professional interior detail resets all of it. Steam cleaning sanitizes every hard surface. Hot water extraction pulls embedded stains, odors, and buildup from carpet and fabric seats that a vacuum and surface wipe have never touched. Andrew finishes with leather conditioning on any leather surfaces, dressed hard plastics, and streak-free interior glass.

Our Process

1

Pre-Clean Assessment

Andrew walks through the interior before starting, notes problem areas, and confirms the scope of work so there are no surprises.

2

Dry Extraction

Full vacuum of all surfaces, crevices, under seats, and trunk. Pet hair removed with specialized tools before any wet work begins.

3

Steam Treatment

High-temperature steam applied to all hard surfaces. Kills bacteria, loosens grime, and sanitizes without chemicals.

4

Stain Pre-Treatment

Individual stains treated with targeted products before the main extraction pass. Giving treatments time to work produces dramatically better results.

5

Hot Water Extraction

Professional extractor used on all carpets, floor mats, and fabric seats. Hot water and cleaning solution injected into fibers, immediately extracted along with dissolved dirt and stains.

6

Surface Detail and Dress

All hard surfaces wiped down and dressed. Leather cleaned and conditioned. Glass cleaned streak-free. Final inspection before calling the job done.
