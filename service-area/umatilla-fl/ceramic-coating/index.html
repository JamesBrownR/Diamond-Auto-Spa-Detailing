Diamond Auto Spa is based in Eustis, just down SR-19 from Umatilla, and travels to Umatilla for full-service appointments. Andrew arrives at your property with a complete professional setup and handles everything on-site. No drop-off, no shop visit.

Umatilla vehicles face paint threats specific to the citrus grove and agricultural corridor environment that ceramic coating is well suited to address. Citrus pollen from the surrounding groves settles onto paint surfaces constantly and contains mild acids that etch into unprotected clear coat when baked in by Florida heat and left between washes. Tree sap from the forest edge along CR-450 bonds to paint surfaces and hardens quickly in the heat. Bug residue from driving through agricultural stretches of SR-19 is similarly acidic and bonds hard to front panels. Florida UV operates on all of it continuously on vehicles parked in open rural driveways.

Ceramic coating bonds to the clear coat at a molecular level and creates a hard, chemically resistant surface layer above it. Citrus pollen, sap, and bug residue that land on a coated surface sit on top of the coating rather than bonding into the clear coat. They become significantly easier to remove at the next wash and cause far less damage in the meantime. UV is blocked before it reaches the paint underneath. The hydrophobic properties of the coating also make the vehicle much easier to keep clean between washes, which matters for vehicles that deal with agricultural road dust and pollen on a regular basis.

Every coating job includes full decontamination and paint correction before the product goes on. Ceramic coating locks in whatever the paint looks like at time of application. Getting the paint right before the coating goes on is non-negotiable.

Cure Period Guidance

Our Process

1

Paint Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint under proper lighting to identify the level of correction needed before coating. Sets the time expectation for the full job before any work begins.

2

Full Decontamination

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar on all painted surfaces. Citrus pollen residue, tree sap, and bug residue lifted completely. No coating can bond correctly to contaminated paint. This stage cannot be skipped.

3

Paint Correction

Machine polish removes swirl marks, pollen etching, water spots, and oxidation. Level of correction matched to paint condition. Since the coating preserves whatever the paint looks like underneath, getting the paint right first is the most important part of the whole job.

4

IPA Wipe Down

All painted panels wiped with isopropyl alcohol to remove any remaining polish oils or residue. Paint inspected one final time under lighting before coating begins. If anything is not right, it gets addressed here.

5

Ceramic Coating Application

Coating applied panel by panel. Each section worked in small areas, allowed to flash, then leveled with a clean suede cloth. Work proceeds in a logical order across the entire vehicle.

6

Cure and Handoff

Andrew walks through cure requirements and maintenance instructions before leaving. Following the cure protocol correctly is what separates a coating that lasts 2 to 5 years from one that degrades in months.
