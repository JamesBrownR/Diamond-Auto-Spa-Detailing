Exterior Detailing for Umatilla Vehicles

Diamond Auto Spa is based in Eustis, just down SR-19 from Umatilla, and travels to Umatilla for full-service appointments. Andrew arrives at your property with a complete professional setup and handles everything on-site. No drop-off, no shop visit.

Umatilla vehicles deal with paint contamination specific to the citrus grove and forest edge environment along SR-19 and CR-450. Citrus pollen from the surrounding groves settles onto paint surfaces constantly and contains mild acids that etch into clear coat when baked in by Florida heat and left between washes. Tree sap from the forest edge along CR-450 bonds to paint and hardens quickly. Bug residue from driving through agricultural stretches of SR-19 is similarly acidic when it dries and bonds hard to front panels and hoods. Unpaved county roads generate fine dust that acts as an abrasive on paint over time. Florida UV operates on all of it uninterrupted on vehicles parked in open rural driveways with inconsistent shade coverage.

A full exterior detail removes all of it properly. Clay bar decontamination lifts the bonded organic layer that washing leaves behind. Machine polishing corrects the paint defects that developed underneath. A paint sealant or wax applied at the end creates a protective barrier against the ongoing agricultural environment.

Our Process

1

Pre-Wash Assessment

Andrew inspects the paint and notes pollen etching, sap bonding, oxidation, and scratch depth. Sets expectations for what the detail will achieve and identifies areas that need extra attention before work begins.

2

Pre-Rinse and Wheel Work

Vehicle rinsed to remove loose debris. Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned first while iron remover dwells on painted surfaces. Prevents cross-contamination from brake dust onto clean paint.

3

Hand Wash

Full two-bucket hand wash using pH-neutral soap and microfiber wash media. Panels washed top to bottom and dried with clean microfiber towels to avoid water spots.

4

Decontamination

Iron remover applied to all painted panels. Clay bar worked across the entire paint surface to lift citrus pollen, tree sap, bug residue, and road dust that washing leaves behind. Paint tested by touch before moving on.

5

Machine Polish

Dual-action polisher removes swirl marks, light scratches, and oxidation panel by panel. Compound and pad selection matched to paint condition. Any areas needing additional passes addressed before moving forward.

6

Protection and Finish

Paint sealant or wax applied to all painted surfaces. Trim dressed. Glass cleaned. Final inspection under proper lighting to confirm results before the job is called complete.
