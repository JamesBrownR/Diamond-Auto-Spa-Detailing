Our Process

1

Pre-Clean Assessment

Andrew walks through the interior before starting, notes problem areas including heavy staining, pet hair, and odor sources, and confirms the scope of work so there are no surprises.

2

Dry Extraction

Full vacuum of all surfaces, crevices, under seats, and trunk. Specialized tools used to lift pet hair before any wet work begins. Starting dry prevents wet contamination from spreading during the cleaning stages.

3

Steam Treatment

High-temperature steam applied to all hard surfaces. Kills bacteria, loosens grime, and sanitizes without chemicals. Particularly effective in vehicles where citrus pollen and agricultural dust have settled into vents and trim crevices over time.

4

Stain Pre-Treatment

Individual stains treated with targeted products and allowed to dwell. Citrus pollen staining and soil from unpaved grove roads need proper contact time before extraction can pull them fully from carpet and fabric fibers.

5

Hot Water Extraction

Professional extractor worked through all carpets, seats, and mats. Multiple passes on heavily soiled areas. The extractor injects and immediately recovers, lifting dissolved contamination out of the fibers rather than spreading it around.

6

Surface Wipe-Down and Dress

All hard surfaces wiped clean and dressed. Leather conditioned. Glass cleaned streak-free. Door jambs and pockets finished. Final inspection before calling the job complete.
