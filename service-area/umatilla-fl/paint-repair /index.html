Umatilla vehicles accumulate paint defects from a specific combination of conditions tied to the agricultural and forest environment along the SR-19 corridor. Citrus pollen from the surrounding groves settles onto paint surfaces constantly and contains acids that etch into clear coat when baked in by Florida heat and left between washes. Tree sap from the forest edge along CR-450 leaves bonded resin deposits that washing alone cannot remove. Bug residue from driving through agricultural stretches of SR-19 is similarly acidic when it dries and bonds hard to the front of the vehicle. Unpaved county roads generate fine dust that acts as a mild abrasive over time. Florida UV operates on all of it uninterrupted on open rural properties.

Paint repair addresses the full combination. Clay bar decontamination before polishing removes the bonded organic residue that has accumulated between washes. Machine polishing then levels the damaged outer layer of clear coat and restores the reflective surface underneath. Andrew completes every job with a full inspection under proper lighting before calling it done.

What's Included

✓
Full Decontamination Wash

Hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar before any polishing begins. Contaminated paint causes new scratches when polished. This step cannot be skipped.

✓
Paint Thickness Measurement

Clear coat thickness measured across all panels before starting. Identifies thin areas from previous polishing or body repair. Guides how aggressively each area can be worked safely.

✓
Paint Defect Assessment

Paint inspected under proper lighting to map swirl marks, scratches, water etching, and oxidation. Determines the number of repair stages required and sets accurate expectations before work begins.

✓
Multi-Stage Machine Polishing

Dual-action or rotary polisher with cutting compound followed by progressively finer polish stages. Each stage removes the marks left by the previous one. Number of stages matched to paint condition.

✓
Panel-by-Panel Work

Repair performed one panel at a time with machine set to the correct speed and pressure for each area. Adjusted throughout rather than running a single pass across the whole car.

✓
Final Inspection Under Lighting

Completed paint inspected under dedicated lighting at multiple angles. Any remaining defects addressed before the job is called done.

✓
Paint Protection Application

Repaired paint finished with a paint sealant or wax. If ceramic coating is being applied as a follow-on service, IPA wipe-down is performed instead and coating follows.

Repair Stages Explained

1

Single-Stage Repair

One pass with a cutting compound removes light swirl marks, minor water spots, and light oxidation. Suitable for well-maintained vehicles or as prep for ceramic coating on paint in reasonable condition.

2

Two-Stage Repair

Heavier cutting compound addresses deeper etching from citrus pollen acids and sap resin, moderate UV oxidation, and water spot damage from Florida afternoon storms baking mineral deposits into the clear coat. Finishing polish refines the surface. The most common approach for Umatilla vehicles that have accumulated a season or more of grove corridor contamination.

3

Multi-Stage Repair

Three or more stages for heavily defected paint, severe oxidation, or deeply neglected vehicles. Rural vehicles that have gone years without professional paint care often need this level of work. Andrew has spent a full day on a single repair job when the paint required it.
