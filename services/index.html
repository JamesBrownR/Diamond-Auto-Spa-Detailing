Professional Mobile Detailing Services

Diamond Auto Spa is a fully mobile operation based in Eustis, FL. Andrew arrives at your home, office, or wherever your vehicle is parked, fully equipped with professional polishers, steam cleaners, hot water extractors, and everything else needed to complete the job. Every service below is performed on-site. There is no shop to drop your vehicle off at because one is not needed.

Central Florida vehicles face some of the most demanding conditions in the country. Intense UV year-round, humidity, hard water, pollen, road tar, and heat that accelerates every form of paint degradation. The services below are designed specifically with those conditions in mind, using products and techniques that hold up in Florida's climate rather than just looking good on the day of the detail.

All Services

Interior Detailing

Steam cleaning, hot water extraction, leather conditioning, odor removal, and a full interior restoration. Every surface inside the vehicle addressed. Pet hair, stains, and years of buildup removed.

Exterior Detailing

Two-bucket hand wash, iron decontamination, clay bar treatment, machine polish, and paint sealant or carnauba wax. The full process that goes far beyond a standard wash.

Ceramic Coating

Professional-grade SiO2 coating that chemically bonds to your clear coat. Years of UV protection, hydrophobic water repellency, and resistance to contamination and oxidation. The strongest paint protection available.

View Service Details →

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing to remove swirl marks, scratches, water etching, and oxidation from the clear coat. The only real fix for defected paint, and required before any ceramic coating application.

View Service Details →

Headlight Restoration

Wet sanding, machine polishing, and UV sealant to restore yellowed or hazy headlight lenses. Restores clarity and improves night visibility at a fraction of replacement cost. Florida UV destroys plastic lenses fast.

View Service Details →

Engine Bay Detail

Safe degreasing, agitation, rinsing, and dressing of all engine bay surfaces. Removes years of grease and road grime. Makes spotting leaks easier and looks sharp under the hood.

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

Call or text and Andrew will walk you through it. Most customers start with a description of what is bothering them about their vehicle and get a straightforward recommendation with no pressure. If your paint is dull and scratched, that is a different conversation than if your interior smells or your headlights are foggy. The right service depends on what your specific vehicle needs.

For vehicles that need everything, a full interior and exterior detail combined with paint correction and ceramic coating is a single appointment that transforms the car completely. Andrew has spent full days doing exactly that on vehicles that came in looking rough and left looking like they just rolled off the lot.
