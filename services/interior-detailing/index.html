What Is Interior Detailing?

Interior detailing is a deep, systematic cleaning of every surface inside your vehicle. It goes far beyond a standard vacuum and wipe-down. Andrew uses professional steam cleaners, hot water extractors, and specialized tools to lift embedded dirt, stains, pet hair, and odors from every part of your interior. carpets, seats, headliner, door panels, dashboard, console, and all the tight spaces in between.

Florida interiors take a beating. The combination of heat, humidity, sand tracked in from the beach, food and drink spills, kids, pets, and daily use leaves most car interiors in a state that regular cleaning can not address. A professional interior detail restores your cabin to a condition most people have not seen since the day they drove off the lot.

Every interior detail is performed mobile. Andrew arrives at your home, office, or wherever your vehicle is parked, fully equipped. No drop-off, no waiting rooms, no arranging a ride home.

Our Process

1

Pre-Clean Assessment

Andrew walks through the interior before starting, notes any problem areas. heavy staining, pet hair, odor sources. and confirms the scope of work so there are no surprises.

2

Dry Extraction

Full vacuum of all surfaces, crevices, under seats, and trunk. Pet hair removed with specialized tools before any wet work begins. Loose debris cleared completely.

3

Steam Treatment

High-temperature steam applied to all hard surfaces. dashboard, console, door panels, vents, buttons, and trim. Kills bacteria, loosens grime, and sanitizes without chemicals.

4

Stain Pre-Treatment

Individual stains on carpet and upholstery treated with targeted products before the main extraction pass. Giving treatments time to work produces dramatically better results.

5

Hot Water Extraction

Professional extractor used on all carpets, floor mats, and fabric seats. Hot water and cleaning solution injected into fibers, immediately extracted along with dissolved dirt and stains.

6

Surface Detail & Dress

All hard surfaces wiped down and dressed. Leather cleaned and conditioned. Glass cleaned streak-free inside. Final inspection to make sure nothing was missed.

Common Interior Problems We Fix

These are the situations where customers call us most often. If your interior has any of these issues, professional extraction and steam cleaning is the most effective solution available.

Pet hair and dander. Embedded in carpet fibers and upholstery in a way that vacuuming alone cannot reach. We use a combination of specialized removal tools and extraction to pull it out completely.

Food and drink stains. Old set-in stains from coffee, soda, milk, and food spills that have been sitting for months or years. Pre-treatment combined with hot water extraction gets results that look impossible beforehand.

Smoke odor. Cigarette smoke bonds to every surface in the interior. Steam cleaning and odor eliminator treatment addresses it at the source rather than masking it with fragrance.

Mold and mildew. Common in Florida vehicles, especially those that have had wet items left inside or a window left open in the rain. Steam treatment kills mold spores and extraction removes the residue.

Used vehicle condition. Buying used often means inheriting someone else's years of buildup. An interior detail at the start resets the baseline and gives you a clean foundation to maintain from.
